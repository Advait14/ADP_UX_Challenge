# User Journeys

## Primary journey — recruiter review

1. Recruiter opens **Today’s work**.
2. They select **Needs your decision** from the action-first worklist.
3. They select a candidate and see their current status, evidence found, missing evidence, confidence, and suggested next step.
4. They open the full candidate review to verify job-related source evidence.
5. They choose to request clarification, send an assessment, shortlist, do not progress, or escalate for human review.
6. The recruiter records an override reason where needed.
7. A candidate update is drafted and sent only after recruiter approval.
8. The decision and approval are added to the timeline.

## Edge-case journey — low-confidence candidate

1. Rohan applies with retail customer-service experience but no stated CRM experience.
2. The system finds transferable issue-resolution evidence but reports low confidence and missing information.
3. The system does not reject Rohan automatically.
4. The recruiter verifies the CV excerpt and approves a short, accessible customer-response simulation.
5. The assessment adds role-relevant evidence for the recruiter’s next decision.

## Hiring-manager handoff

1. Recruiter shortlists a candidate and records their rationale.
2. Hiring manager sees only the candidate summary, agreed criteria, assessment signal, and recruiter rationale.
3. Hiring manager approves the next interview step or requests more information.
