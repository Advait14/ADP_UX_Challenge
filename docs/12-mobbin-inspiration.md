# Mobbin Inspiration

The prototype borrows interaction patterns, not visual assets or exact layouts.

## Patterns applied

- **Filterable candidate worklist:** role context, stage counts, and efficient list scanning, observed in [Deel](https://mobbin.com/screens/4f007485-06fc-4eb0-9ab2-8c700b31e148).
- **Progressive disclosure:** candidate list alongside a detailed candidate panel, observed in [Employment Hero](https://mobbin.com/screens/3e752312-ccfe-47ba-b218-26287519e291).
- **Criteria-based candidate summary:** clear matched / partial / missing criteria near the candidate profile, observed in [Workable](https://mobbin.com/screens/4b78a9ef-a917-406e-8deb-ae5aa8523924).
- **Stage visibility:** pipeline framing as a supporting navigation model, observed in [Wellfound](https://mobbin.com/screens/c34d9faa-2fbd-4eaa-88fe-111cb07f378c).
- **Explicit candidate actions:** clear operational actions such as shortlist or decline, observed in [Pin](https://mobbin.com/screens/5a8800a2-0136-4d9e-a0e5-07d4ba3547f9).

## Adaptation for this concept

The concept does not use a kanban board as the primary review tool because high-volume, evidence-led screening benefits more from a prioritised worklist. It adds responsible-AI safeguards that the visual references alone do not establish: advisory recommendations, low-confidence escalation, explanation of inputs, recruiter approval, and audit history.
