# Validation Plan

## Purpose

Test whether recruiters can understand the recommendation, recognize uncertainty, verify evidence, and make a defensible decision quickly.

## Lightweight usability test

Recruit 3–5 participants with recruiting, HR, hiring, or operational-review experience. If recruitment professionals are unavailable, label feedback from others as directional only.

## Task

> You are screening candidates for a Customer Support Associate role. Review Rohan and decide what should happen next. Explain what the AI recommendation means and why you chose that outcome.

## Observe whether participants can

- Find the next action in the worklist
- Understand why the recommendation is low confidence
- Locate source evidence and missing evidence
- Avoid interpreting the score as an automatic decision
- Choose an assessment, clarification, shortlist, or escalation
- Find how to preview or approve a candidate update

## Measures

- Time to reach a decision
- Decision confidence and rationale quality
- Number of incorrect interpretations of AI authority
- Success rate finding evidence and missing information
- Qualitative feedback on clarity and cognitive load

## Iteration triggers

- If people treat the score as a verdict, reduce its prominence further.
- If people miss missing evidence, strengthen the evidence/gaps separation.
- If people cannot identify the next action, simplify the queue row and decision controls.
