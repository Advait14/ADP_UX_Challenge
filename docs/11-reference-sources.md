# Reference Sources

These sources informed design considerations. They do not validate the fictional prototype or make legal claims about XYZ Inc.

1. [NIST AI Risk Management Framework — FAQs](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework/ai-risk-management-framework-faqs)
   - Trustworthy-AI characteristics including transparency, explainability, privacy, accountability, and harmful-bias management.

2. [NIST AI RMF Core](https://airc.nist.gov/airmf-resources/airmf/5-sec-core/)
   - Defined human-AI roles, documentation, and risk-management practices.

3. [U.S. EEOC and Department of Justice warning on disability discrimination and AI](https://www.eeoc.gov/newsroom/us-eeoc-and-department-justice-warn-against-disability-discrimination)
   - Informs the design consideration for accommodation and alternative-assessment routes; not legal advice for this concept.

4. [Greenhouse: 2023 Candidate Interview Experience Report summary](https://www.greenhouse.com/blog/candidates-cautious-of-employer-red-flags-results-from-the-2023-greenhouse-candidate-interview-experience-report)

5. Vedant’s Summer Internship Program report, supplied for this assessment.
   - Informs the practitioner context around screening, scorecards, stakeholder alignment, and recruiting workflow.
