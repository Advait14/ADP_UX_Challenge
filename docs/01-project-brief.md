# Project Brief

## Goal

Help a recruiter screen high volumes of Customer Support Associate applicants faster, while preserving explainability and recruiter approval for consequential outcomes.

## Product promise

AI organizes approved, job-related evidence and recommends a next step. The recruiter reviews, approves, overrides, or escalates the recommendation.

## Users

- **Recruiter:** primary user; reviews candidates and records screening decisions.
- **Candidate:** receives a clear, recruiter-approved status update or request for information.
- **Hiring manager:** receives a concise recruiter-reviewed shortlist brief.

## In scope

Candidate intake, evidence-led screening, a low-confidence assessment route, recruiter decision/override, candidate communication preview, and an audit timeline.

## Out of scope

Interview scheduling, offers, onboarding, ATS integrations, and complete candidate relationship management.

## Success measures

- Recruiter time per candidate review
- Time to first candidate update
- Recommendation override rate and reason capture
- Shortlist progression rate
- Completion rate for recruiter-approved candidate updates
