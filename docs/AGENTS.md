# Project Guidance

## Purpose

This workspace supports a UX-assessment prototype for AI-assisted high-volume candidate screening.

## Non-negotiable product principles

- AI recommends; recruiters decide.
- Show job-related evidence and the next action before a score.
- Low confidence, conflicting evidence, and accommodation needs route to human review; they do not trigger automatic rejection.
- Do not claim that the prototype sends real messages, processes real applicant data, or uses a real AI model.
- Keep hiring-manager access limited to a concise shortlist brief.

## Scope

Prioritise the screening flow: worklist → candidate evidence → recruiter decision → candidate update → audit history. Do not expand this prototype into a full ATS, scheduling, onboarding, or production backend.

## Evidence discipline

Separate assessment facts, practitioner input, research findings, assumptions, and future implementation considerations. Cite external sources when making a research-backed claim.
